Preparing a Pet-Safe Home

How to spot and remove common hazards before your pet arrives.

Bringing a new pet home is exciting, but it can also be stressful for them. A little preparation goes a long way toward helping your pet feel safe and settle in quickly.

Start by setting up a quiet space with a bed, fresh water, food and a litter tray or toys. Keep the first few days calm, limit visitors, and let your pet explore at their own pace.

Stick to a routine for feeding, play and sleep. Pets feel secure when their day is predictable, and a steady routine makes it easier to spot changes in appetite or behavior.

If something does not feel right, such as a pet that refuses to eat for more than a day or hides constantly, book a check-up with a vet. Early advice can prevent small problems from growing.

Finally, be patient. Every pet adjusts at a different speed. With time, patience and plenty of positive attention, your new companion will soon feel at home.
